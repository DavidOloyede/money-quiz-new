Section 5

Moments in motion

The still drawings in Section 3, moving. Every move starts and ends on its pose, so with motion off Omer simply stands in it. Tap any figure to replay it, and switch to Still to see the rest state that reduce motion shows.

Defaults to your device’s reduce-motion setting.

5.1Omer’s moves

One move per pose and per outfit. In the app marks the moves the app already plays (Omer pops in, then breathes, hops or wobbles as one piece, from src/celebrate.css). Spec marks the part-by-part moves proposed here: an arm, a prop or the cape moves while the rest of him stays put.

The eight poses

The wardrobe

Each outfit’s move says what the moment is about: the Coach trains, the Wrangler rounds up, the Builder builds. The Detective now stands up straight, and the Coach’s sweatband and wristbands and the Wrangler’s bandana wear the app’s forest green.

  • Pivots are joints. Arms swing from the shoulder, props from the hand, hats from the rim, so nothing detaches at any frame.
  • Small amplitudes. Limbs turn 6–16°, the body squashes 3–6%. Bigger reads as frantic at 56px.
  • Rest on the pose. Every loop passes through the drawing in 3.3; reduce motion shows exactly that frame, with no particles.
  • A moment, not a screensaver. In the app, celebration loops run a few times and settle; only idle breathing keeps going.

5.2The Hero in flight

Only the Hero flies. He holds the flying pose while the world moves past him: clouds drift at three speeds, wind and manna stream by, his cape ripples in the wind and the speed lines trail behind. He appears for Debt Slayer and at every level-up from level 8.

Proposed

The debt-paid-off party isn’t built yet. This is a sketch: the Hero loops, a comic burst lands and confetti pops.

5.3The streak moment

A port of StreakOverlay.tsx. Day 7 is the first goal day: “ONE WEEK!” in a comic burst, a turning sunburst, confetti, and Omer as the Coach beside the flame; the day count flips from 6 to 7 and the seventh pip pops in. Day 7 also earns the Week of Diligence badge. An ordinary day stays flame-only, with a ten-second countdown under “Keep going”.

The Coach’s barbell reps are the proposed move from 5.1; the app hops him today. Goal days: 7, 14, 30, 60, 100 and 180 days, then one year and every year after.

5.4Level up

A port of MilestoneOverlay.tsx. The sunburst turns, Omer jumps and glows while manna falls into his rim, “LEVEL UP!” lands, the level number flips and the new title flips in with a shine. Pick a level to see who shows up.

5.5The level ladder

Nine titles that follow the stewardship arc of Luke 16:10 and the parable of the talents. Reaching level n takes 50 × n × (n − 1) XP in total, so each level asks 100 XP more than the last. The level card shows the number on a forest tile; the title changes with every level, and only two levels bring an outfit.

XP comes from showing up and learning: 10 for the day’s check-in plus a streak bonus of 2 a day (up to 30), 10 for the question of the day (+5 when right), 10 per right quiz answer, 15 for finishing a quiz, 25 for a perfect one, 25 for an import. Source: packages/core/lib/gamification.ts.

5.6Badges and their outfits

Seven badges, earned once each. The badge moment spins the medal in with a shine and shows Omer in the badge’s outfit; when several badges with different outfits arrive together he stands proud instead. Badges are emoji and a name today; drawn medals are still to come.
