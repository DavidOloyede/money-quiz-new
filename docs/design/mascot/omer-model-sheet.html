Omer, now with legs and a wardrobe

The pedestal foot is gone: Omer stands on two stubby legs built just like his arms. And he has nine outfits, each tied to a real moment in the app, so the costume tells you what’s happening before anyone reads a word.

●Not in Figma yet. Figma’s Starter plan allows 20 tool calls a month, and building the design system used them up. This page is the review copy; the Figma update is written and ready to run.

How the legs are drawn

Short, straight and simple: a pose is just a bend. Measurements use the rim width as one unit, the same as the rest of the model sheet.

Build
Exactly like the arms: a 7px honey limb inside a 14px ink outline, round ends.
Placement
Hips 0.15 rim-widths apart, tucked under the bowl so the tops hide behind it.
Length
About 0.16 rim-widths of leg showing, from the bowl base to the ground.
Feet
A tiny rounded nub that turns outward. No toes, no shoes.
Keep it simple
No knees and no joints: straight, noodle-simple limbs, so every pose is just a bend.

The eight poses, with legs

The legs carry as much of the feeling as the arms: wide when cheering, crossed when thinking, knees in for “oops”.

Idle

Gentle breathing. Loading screens, the level card.

Wave hello

Welcome, sign-in success, first visit. One foot taps.

Cheer

Right answer, quiz finished. Feet planted wide.

Jump

Level up. Legs dangle, the shadow shrinks.

Thinking

Loading, a question, “working on it”. Legs crossed.

Oops

Not quite. Knees in, still smiling.

Proud

New badge, pinned on the lower right of the bowl. Arms on hips.

Giving

Generosity: holding out a flake.

Omer’s wardrobe

Base Omer stays the default. Earned outfits unlock with a badge or level and then appear in that moment. Contextual outfits show up with matching content, no unlock needed. The cape is the recurring superhero look, and only the Hero flies.

Wardrobe rules

One look
One outfit at a time: at most a hat, one or two wearables and one prop.
One meaning
Each outfit means one kind of moment. Don’t borrow the Chef for a savings win because it’s cute.
Face first
Outfits never cover the eyes or the smile. The Detective’s lens magnifies; it never hides. Hats sit on the rim and may cover the manna heap.
Same hand
Palette colors only, the same ink outline, one cel shade. Simple checks, dots or stripes at most.
Silhouette
Every outfit reads in silhouette at 96px. Below 64px, use base Omer.
Calm money
Outfits appear in moments, lesson cards, badges and empty states, never next to amounts in tables or budgets.
Never preachy
No clerical costumes or religious symbols as outfits. Generosity is shown by giving, not by vestments.
