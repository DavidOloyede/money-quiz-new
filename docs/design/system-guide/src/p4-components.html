Section 2

Component library

Every component is built the same way: a cream or colored face, a 1px hairline if it only holds content or a solid darker edge if it can be pressed, a radius from the scale, and type from one of the three voices. Learn the button and you know the answer tile, the chip and the icon tile.

2.1Buttons

Primary buttons press in. A 4px edge in a darker step of the face sits along the bottom; pressing drops the face 3px while the edge shrinks to 1px, so the button feels physical and nothing around it moves. The shared utilities are btn-primary, btn-secondary, btn-reward and btn-danger in src/index.css.

  • Pressed: the face drops 3px (2px for secondary) and the edge shrinks to 1px over 150ms. Release springs back.
  • Focus: a 2px forest-500 outline with a 2px offset (honey-400 in dark mode), on keyboard focus only, never removed.

  • One primary per view. Pair it with a secondary, never with a second primary. Full width in forms and on phones.
  • Labels are verbs in sentence case: "Start the quiz", not "START" or "Submit".

2.6Quiz screen

A quiz screen has three zones: the progress header (count, in-a-row flame, score), the question card, and the verdict that appears after a pick. Try it: pick an answer with a click or the 1–4 keys, then press Enter or Next. The numbers are sample data.
